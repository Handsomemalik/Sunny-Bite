A small kitchen with a big idea

What began as weekend bake drops and herbal infusions in a vintage garden shed has bloomed into MAVORA. We wanted to cultivate a serene escape where breakfast lingers into afternoon laughter.

Every dressing, pastry crust, and seasonal compote is whipped up in-house each morning using edible florals and produce sourced directly from heritage family growers within fifty miles.
