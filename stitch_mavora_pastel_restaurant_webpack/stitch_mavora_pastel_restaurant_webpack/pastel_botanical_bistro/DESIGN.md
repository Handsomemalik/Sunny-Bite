## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a cheerful, gourmet cafe atmosphere: warm, artisanal, welcoming, and refined. Balancing a "happy food" emotional signature with upscale dining sensibilities, the aesthetic merges organic warmth, soft pastel hues, and sophisticated typography. 

The visual style unites contemporary soft-touch tactile design and frosted glass elements. Airy layouts, pill-shaped interactions, deep botanical contrast, and buttery cream foundations create a comforting, indulgent, and appetizing digital environment.

## Colors

The palette relies on delicate pastry tones anchored by deep, botanical greens to ensure high legibility and an elevated tone:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Primary background is warm cream (`#FBF5EB`). Elevated containers and cards use pure cream tint (`#FDFAF3`). Secondary sections and structural footers deploy soft garden mints (`#E8F0E4` and `#EBF2E8`).
- **Brand Accents & CTAs:** Primary interactive buttons and highlights use raspberry rose (`#E6809A`), supported by a soft pink wash (`#F9E4EA`). Secondary structural buttons utilize forest herb green (`#2F5D3E`).
- **Typography:** Display titles use dark botanical green (`#243022`). Body copy uses balanced sage green (`#5F6B5E`), while captions and metadata settle into muted sage (`#8A938A`).
- **Illustrative & Icon Accents:** Playful circular icon backplates rotate between sweet pea mint (`#CFE8D5`), whipped butter yellow (`#F6E3A6`), and macaroon pink (`#F7C9D8`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial, artisanal rhythm. Editorial headlines leverage the graceful curves of Playfair Display with medium (500) weights and tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`), evoking high-end cafe menus and literary bistros. Functional elements, menus, descriptions, and UI controls rely on Inter for pristine readability at micro and standard sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid orchestrates desktop screens with a max-width container of 1280px, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile. Generous vertical section rhythm (`space-xl`) permits cards, menus, and imagery to breathe against the warm cream canvas.

Card internals strictly observe internal breathing room (`space-md` to `space-lg`), ensuring photographic elements never touch outer borders and preserving an unhurried, comfortable composition.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cool, synthetic gray drop shadows in favor of warm, sunlit cafe ambiance:

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Card surfaces (`#FDFAF3`) feature ultra-soft, diffused shadows tinted with deep umber and amber tones: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(60, 45, 30, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(60, 45, 30, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Glass Pill (Navigation):** The floating navigation bar utilizes translucent cream glass: `background: rgba(251, 245, 235, 0.78); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);` paired with a delicate, sub-perceptual rim: `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Floating Active Elements:** Hover states on interactive cards scale gently (`scale: 1.015`) while blooming shadow spread slightly: `0 18px 40px -6px rgba(60, 45, 30, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, rounded silhouette language:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** All actionable elements—primary buttons, secondary buttons, category chips, status tags, floating navigation bars, and decorative icon bases—use a continuous pill shape (9999px border-radius).
- **Cards & Plates (`rounded-3xl` / 28px):** Structural content wrappers, dessert display cards, reservation modules, and menu containers feature sweeping 28px outer radii.
- **Embedded Media (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Inner food photography, maps, and nested containers are nested inside cards with 16px radii, creating a harmonious concentric contour with the parent border.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pink Pill):** Background `#E6809A`, text `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded. Hover shifts toward `#D9708B` with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Forest Pill):** Background `#2F5D3E`, text `#FFFFFF`, fully rounded. Hover deepens to `#264B32`.
- **Soft Accent Button:** Background `#F9E4EA`, text `#E6809A`, hover background `#F4D3DC`.
- **Metrics:** Heights of 48px (large) and 40px (regular) with horizontal padding of `space-lg`.

### Cards & Menu Tiles
- **Surface:** `#FDFAF3` with 28px border-radius and warm ambient shadow.
- **Padding:** Continuous 20px–24px internal padding frame.
- **Internal Media:** Imagery occupies the top or side with a 16px radius, separated cleanly from typographic metadata without full edge bleeds.
- **Dietary Badges:** Floated atop imagery or inline using 9999px pills in `#EBF2E8` with `#2F5D3E` typography.

### Chips & Category Selectors
- **Unselected:** Subtle border `1px solid #E8E2D5`, text `#5F6B5E`, background transparent or `#FDFAF3`.
- **Selected:** Filled `#2F5D3E` with white text, or filled `#E6809A` for promotional categories.
- **Icon Circles:** Fixed 40px or 48px circular avatars in `#CFE8D5`, `#F6E3A6`, or `#F7C9D8` hosting single botanical or culinary vector icons.

### Navigation Bar
- **Pill Glass Frame:** Positioned fixed top, floating centered with a maximum width, 32px vertical safe margin, `rounded-full`, 12px vertical padding, 24px horizontal padding, backed by frosted `#FBF5EB` glass and micro white rim.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Background `#FDFAF3`, border `1.5px solid #EADDCF`, rounded `rounded-full` for search and single-line inputs; `rounded-2xl` for text areas. Focus ring radiates soft `#E6809A` with 0 0 0 3px `#F9E4EA`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded circular indicators (`rounded-full`) filled with `#2F5D3E` when checked, bordered with `#5F6B5E` when inactive.
